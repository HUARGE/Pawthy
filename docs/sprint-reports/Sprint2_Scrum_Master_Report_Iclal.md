- **GitHub workflow:** The team reiterated that everyone should work on a separate branch for their own tasks.
- **Scrum Master documentation:** The Scrum Master noted the reporting and follow-up work that still needed to be finished before submission.

## 8. Communication and Coordination

The 22 November meeting was useful because the team could discuss the work together instead of reviewing each part separately. This helped identify small design issues and clarify the next steps. The member who missed the call was informed about the discussion afterward.

The Scrum Master kept the discussion focused on what was finished, what still needed work, and who should follow up. Since some updates were shared verbally, this report describes them as reported progress rather than independently verified results.

## 9. Scrum Master Contributions

The Scrum Master’s main contributions in Sprint 2 included organizing the progress review meeting, helping the team discuss feedback, tracking open tasks, and following up on updates after the meeting. The Scrum Master also reinforced the expectation that each team member should use an individual GitHub branch.

The Scrum Master prepared this report to document what the team discussed, what changed afterward, and which decisions still need attention. This work focused on keeping the team coordinated and making its progress easier to follow.

## 10. Reflection and Improvements for the Next Sprint

Overall, the meeting helped the team spot issues before the deadline and gave everyone a clearer view of the sprint's progress. At the same time, some decisions, such as the logo and tutorial, were left open, and not every follow-up had a confirmed completion status.

For Sprint 3, shorter and more regular progress updates could help the team identify dependencies earlier and record each decision with a responsible person. Keeping GitHub branches and pull requests organized will also make it easier to see which work is ready for review. These changes could improve coordination and reduce last-minute uncertainty.
